# NOC and Network Operations Glossary

This section is a quick-reference dictionary for common terms used in NOC monitoring, network troubleshooting, ISP coordination, ticketing, and incident management.

> **Confidentiality:** Keep examples generic. Do not include real customer details, internal IP addresses, circuit IDs, ticket references, credentials, or other confidential production information.

## Guide in This Section

1. [NOC Network Glossary](01-NOC-Network-Glossary.md) — definitions and practical distinctions for common networking, monitoring, incident, and service-management terms.

## How to Use This Glossary

- Look up an unfamiliar acronym or term while reading another guide.
- Read the meaning in context rather than memorizing only the full form.
- Check your organization's SOP for terms that have an internal definition.
- Confirm operational status and impact using evidence, not terminology alone.
- Add new terms only when they are useful, verified, and not already covered.

## Main Topics Covered

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| NOC and connectivity | NOC, ISP, ILL, P2P, LAN, WAN |
| Incident communication | ETA, ETR, TETR, RCA, SLA, escalation |
| Link and performance | Link Down, Flapping, Latency, Packet Loss, RTT, Jitter |
| Monitoring and troubleshooting | Alert, Reachability, Ping, DNS, DHCP, Logs |
| Ticket and service management | Priority, Severity, Impact, Urgency, Handover, Resolution |

Acronyms and workflows can vary between organizations. Use the definitions approved by your team, especially for ETA/ETR, TETR, MTTR, priority levels, and SLA terms.
